What Makes Saint Vanity Different

Saint Vanity Sizing and Fit Guide

Sizing is where most first-time buyers go wrong, so here is the simple rule:

  • True size = the intended oversized fit. This is how the pieces are designed to look.
  • Size down one full size = a more tailored, standard fit. Choose this only if you do not want the boxy drape.

Because the fit is oversized by design, ordering two sizes up will not give you a better result — it will just swallow the silhouette. Stick to the rule above, and check the official size guide before ordering if you are between sizes.

The common thread: let one statement piece lead, and keep the rest simple.
